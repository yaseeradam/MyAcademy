# SchoolMS Waitlist Website — Product Brief

## Project Overview
- The SchoolMS Waitlist Website invites schools, districts, and educators to register interest and get early access to our all‑in‑one school management platform.
- Primary goals: clearly communicate value, showcase features, collect qualified leads, and segment them by role to personalize onboarding.
- Outcomes: a growing waitlist, validated demand, and a feedback loop to refine product before general release.

## What We Offer
- Unified platform to run school operations end‑to‑end: administration, academics, attendance, communication, billing, analytics, and reporting.
- Modern, responsive dashboards for school admins, teachers, and parents.
- Secure authentication, role‑based access, and audit trails.
- Real‑time insights, notifications, and messaging that reduce manual work and improve decisions.
- Fast setup, guided onboarding, and data import tools to migrate from spreadsheets or legacy systems.

## Key Features
- Admin & Operations
  - School creation and configuration with role‑based permissions.
  - Attendance tracking (students and teachers), classes, subjects, timetables.
  - Fees and billing workflows, payments integration (roadmap), and financial reports.
  - Audit logs, reports dashboards, and CSV export utilities.
  - User management (admins, teachers, students, parents) with activation/deactivation.
- Teaching & Learning
  - Class management: schedules, homework, exams, grading.
  - Attendance tools optimized for speed and accuracy.
  - Resource library and file uploads for teaching materials.
- Parents & Communication
  - Parent dashboards with attendance, fees, and school updates.
  - Notifications, chats, and announcements to keep families in the loop.
- Analytics & Insights
  - Visual dashboards for performance, attendance, engagement, and trends.
  - Search and filters across people, classes, and logs.
- Platform & Security
  - Session management, secure auth, and role‑based controls.
  - Scalable architecture with modular APIs and real‑time sockets.

## Why Join the Waitlist
- Early access with priority onboarding and dedicated support.
- Founding discounts and flexible pricing options for pilot schools.
- Influence the roadmap by sharing feedback during the beta.
- Access to private community updates and release notes.

## Color Palette (Brand + UI)
- Primary: Indigo `#4F46E5`
- Secondary: Sky `#0EA5E9`
- Accent: Violet `#A78BFA`
- Success: Emerald `#10B981`
- Warning: Amber `#F59E0B`
- Danger: Red `#EF4444`
- Neutral Dark: Gray `#111827`
- Neutral Mid: Gray `#6B7280`
- Neutral Light: Gray `#F3F4F6`
- Background: White `#FFFFFF`

Usage Guidelines
- Primary for CTAs, highlights, and navigation emphasis.
- Secondary for links, hover states, and complementary accents.
- Accent for badges, charts, and decorative gradients.
- Neutrals for typography, cards, and panel backgrounds.
- Maintain AA contrast (≥ 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text).

## Brand Voice
- Clear, trustworthy, and supportive.
- Focus on outcomes (time saved, accuracy, transparency) over features alone.
- Inclusive language for schools of all sizes and types.

## Page Structure (Waitlist Site)
- Hero: value proposition, primary CTA (Join the Waitlist), secondary CTA (Learn More).
- Benefits: 4–6 concise cards that tie features to outcomes.
- Feature Highlights: screenshots or illustrations of admin, teacher, parent views.
- Social Proof: testimonials (placeholder initially) and “trusted by” section (roadmap).
- How It Works: onboarding steps (sign up → configure → invite staff → go live).
- FAQ: pricing, data security, integrations, rollout timeline.
- Signup Form: name, email, role (admin/teacher/parent/other), school name, school size.
- Footer: contact, privacy, terms, and social links.

## Data & Segmentation
- Capture: name, email, role, school name, size, country.
- Segment cohorts for tailored outreach and onboarding playbooks.
- Track conversion events and measure funnel performance.

## Next Steps
- Design wireframe using the color palette and page structure.
- Implement a responsive landing page with form validation.
- Connect the form to an API endpoint to store leads securely.
- Set up email confirmations and a simple onboarding workflow.

- Keep text readable: pair gradients with solid text colors and sufficient contrast.
